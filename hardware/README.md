# Printed capsule parts

Two smooth parts, generated by `make_case.py`:

- `capsule_base.stl` — flat on top, so both sensors stay parallel (the app subtracts their readings to cancel the Earth field). Sensor pockets with corner stops (open on all sides, so the wires leave on whichever edge the module has its pins) and an engraved arrow pointing to the index-finger side (sensor 1); a tray for the battery, the multiplexer and the XIAO stacked, with 2.75 mm on each long side for solder joints and bent wires and room for 2 mm foam tape between the layers; a Voronoi relief on the wings. On the wrist-side wall, a holder for the vibration motor board (21 mm wide, upright in two grooves, motor outwards, 2 mm behind it for the solder joints, wires into the tray through a notch at the top of that wall). Inside the tray at the wrist end, room for the slide switch (body along the fingers; cut off its mounting ears), its lever out through a window in the little-finger-side wall.
- `capsule_saddle.stl` — glued under the base. Its underside follows the convex back of the hand, as an open Voronoi lattice with a solid rim. A channel in its flat face, the full length, carries a 20 mm elastic strap between the saddle and the base: no slots at the ends, so the capsule is 73 mm across the hand.

Print both flat side down, no supports (the saddle with its arc up; its channel roof is a ~21 mm bridge, which PLA bridges fine with normal cooling). PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20% infill.

Before printing:
- **Measure your parts** and put the sizes into `PARTS` (module sizes differ between sellers).
- **Measure your hand** for the saddle: hand relaxed, a straight ruler across the back of the hand where the capsule sits, touching the highest point; the gap under the ruler 40 mm left and right of that point, averaged, goes into `ARCH_DROP_AT_40` (5 mm now is a placeholder).
